# Contract: Cross-Scope And Evidence Boundary

## Cross-Scope Requirements

- source and target scopes must differ
- approved tunnels require authorization refs
- approved tunnels must be sanitized-only
- approved tunnels must require evidence anchoring
- taint exclusion rules must be present

## Evidence Boundary

Memory may explain why VeraCrawl tries a strategy. It must not satisfy publication evidence coverage. Memory-derived strategies must re-anchor to current or selected historical evidence before publication.
